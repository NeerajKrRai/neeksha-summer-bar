## Full description

**Welcome to the Summer Refreshment Bar — where every drink is imaginary and every sip is magic!** ♪( ´∀｀)

Designed by an 8-year-old chef with a very big imagination, this is a pretend-play mixing game where kids craft fantastical summer refreshments out of fruits, herbs, and iced tea — then sip them with two giggling kaomoji friends.

**HOW IT WORKS**
🍉 Open the Fridge and pick 3 fruits for your base
❄️ Raid the Freezer — jelly cubes, jade ice, and a surprise Herb Pack
✨ Decorate at the Garnish Table — butterflies, blossoms, sugar glass, a mini tea bag
🥤 Pick a straw colour for each friend, then tilt your phone to take pretend sips!

**COLLECT & UNLOCK**
📁 Every creation is saved as a beautiful recipe card you can keep or share as a picture
🌸 Make 10 drinks to unlock the Secret Pantry with sparkly VIP ingredients
🛍️ Earn Sakura Glaze petals and spend them in the pretend Sakura Store

**MADE FOR FAMILIES**
✔ 100% make-believe — fruits, herbs and tea, nothing else
✔ No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts.
✔ Collects zero data — the app doesn't even use the internet
✔ Works completely offline: planes, trains, and blanket forts
✔ Everything stays on your device

Built with love by Neeksha (age 8) and her dad. 人(´∀｀ )♪ ～♥
