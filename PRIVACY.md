# Privacy Policy — Neeksha's Summer Bar

**Effective date:** 5 July 2026
**Contact:** neeraj.it3.uk@gmail.com

Neeksha's Summer Bar is a make-believe drink-mixing game designed for children.
All "cocktails" in the app are imaginary and made of pretend fruits, herbs, and
tea — there are no references to real alcoholic drinks.

## The short version

**This app collects no data. None.**

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party
services. The Android app does not even request the internet permission — it
is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere.

## What the app stores (on your device only)

The game saves its progress in the app's own local storage on your device:

- the nickname typed on the welcome screen (e.g. "Chef Starlight"),
- the pretend recipe cards you create,
- the pretend "Sakura Glaze" points and shop items,
- whether the Secret Pantry has been unlocked.

This information never leaves the device. We cannot see it, and neither can
anyone else. Recipe card images you choose to save are written to your
device's downloads/photos by you, and stay under your control.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families
Policy. Because no personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared,
there is nothing for us to store, sell, or delete on any server. The nickname
a child enters exists only inside the app on that device.

## Deleting data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage in your device settings,
permanently removes everything the app ever stored.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above (for example, if
an update added an online feature), this policy will be updated first and the
app's store listing will reflect it.
